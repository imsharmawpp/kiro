Industrial Backbone

Bahadurgarh:
A City on the Rise

Bahadurgarh is not a village on the edge of Delhi. It is the NCR's largest and most diversified industrial hub — home to over 2,800 registered manufacturing units spanning ceramics, glass, electronics, footwear, FMCG, automotive, steel, and chemicals.

Major brands anchored here include HNG Glass, Somany Ceramics, Parle Agro, Yokohama Tyres, and Relaxo Footwear — drawing a skilled, aspirational workforce that needs quality residential options. The 490-acre industrial zone generates continuous demand for real estate that local supply has never adequately served.

When a city has industrial depth, it doesn't just grow — it compounds. Employment attracts workforce. Workforce creates demand. Demand drives appreciation. The Heritage is positioned at the intersection of all three.
